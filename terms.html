2. What Jobbly does — and doesn't do

Jobbly is a browser extension that fills out job application forms using information you provide, and generates suggested answers to screening questions using AI. Jobbly never submits an application without your action — you always review the filled form and click Submit yourself.

You are responsible for the accuracy of everything you submit. AI-generated answers are suggestions based on your profile and the job description — they can be wrong, generic, or not reflect your actual experience accurately. Review every answer before submitting any application. Jobbly is not responsible for the content of applications you choose to submit.

Jobbly does not guarantee interviews, job offers, or any employment outcome.

3. Accounts

You can use Jobbly's core features without an account. Creating an account lets your profile and data sync across devices. You're responsible for keeping your login credentials secure and for all activity under your account.

4. Subscriptions and billing

Jobbly offers a Free plan and paid Beginner and Pro plans, each with a different daily limit on AI-generated answers, billed monthly. Current pricing is shown on our pricing page.

  • Paid subscriptions renew automatically each month until cancelled.
  • Upgrades take effect immediately — you're charged the prorated difference for the remainder of your current billing period, and the full new price from your next renewal.
  • Downgrades take effect at the end of your current billing period — you keep your current plan's access until then, at no additional charge, and the new (lower) price applies from your next renewal.
  • Cancellations can be made any time from the Billing section of the dashboard. You keep access through the end of the period you've already paid for; we don't provide partial refunds for unused time.
  • Payment is processed by Stripe. We don't store your card details.
